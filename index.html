Who We Are

Our Mission

TAMID Group is a nonprofit organization that develops the professional skills of undergraduate students through hands-on work with Israeli startups and companies.

At UCLA, members train in consulting and investing, work directly with real Israeli companies, and join a national network of students and alumni across dozens of universities.

What We Do

Our Programs

01

Education

A new-member curriculum covering consulting frameworks, financial modeling, and the Israeli tech ecosystem.

02

Consulting

Pro-bono projects for Israeli startups, from market sizing to go-to-market strategy, presented to founders.

03

Investment Fund

Hands-on investing in the Israeli stock market with real dollars, backed by equity research and stock pitches.

04

Summer Fellowship

An eight-week internship in Israel with a startup or established company, plus a cohort of TAMID peers.
